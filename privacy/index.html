Privacy Policy

Terehi · Last updated October 16, 2026

Terehi is a private iPhone app for painting supporter flags onto matchday photos of your face and saving those cheer portraits on this device. This policy explains what the app uses. By using Terehi, you agree to this policy and allow Terehi to store the photos and settings described here on this iPhone. You give permission when you continue to use the app after reading this page.

Who we are

Terehi is published as a local iPhone application (bundle ID com.terehi.app). There is no Terehi account, no sign-in, no sign-out, and no Terehi cloud sync. Cheer work stays in this installation. For privacy questions, contact terehi.studio@gmail.com.

Information stored on this iPhone

Depending on how you use the app, Terehi may store the following in its private container on this device:

  • Photos you capture with the camera, plus working copies used to compose a cheer portrait.
  • Face placement choices, team flags, prediction banners, slogans, and drafts you confirm.
  • Rendered cheer files, a local gallery index, and display preferences such as onboarding completion.
  • Your Match Flare balance and a local purchase ledger, including Apple transaction identifiers used so the same purchase is not granted twice.

Terehi does not collect payment card numbers. Card details for in-app purchases are handled by Apple, not by Terehi forms.

Permissions

Terehi asks for system permissions only when you use the related feature. The first launch screen does not request Camera, Photos, or Microphone access. Declining a permission keeps drafts and other studio tools available.

  • Camera — when you tap Start Camera to capture photos of your face so flag paint can follow cheeks and forehead.
  • Photos — when you save a finished cheer photo into your photo library.
  • Microphone — when you turn Sound On so stadium sound can be recorded with a later clip.
  • Notifications — only after onboarding, if you allow optional matchday alerts.

Terehi does not request tracking permission and does not use advertising SDKs to follow you across other apps or websites.

Purchases

Optional Match Flare packs are consumable in-app purchases sold through Apple. Apple processes payment under Apple’s policies. Terehi keeps a local ledger so your Match Flare balance is accurate and the same transaction is not granted twice. Unused Match Flares stay on this installation. They are removed if you delete the app or use Clear All Data. Terehi does not offer a Restore Purchases control because Match Flares are consumable and stored only on this device.

Sharing

Terehi does not sell personal information and does not share your photos or audio with advertising networks. Flag paint, face tracking, and portrait rendering are produced on this iPhone. The app does not send cheer photos, voice recordings, or device identifiers to a third-party image service.

Apple may receive purchase and device information as part of App Store and StoreKit services. If you save a finished photo to Photos, or if you back up this iPhone with iCloud or a computer, those copies follow Apple’s backup and Photos rules. Opening this Privacy Policy or the Terms of Use inside the app loads the page from terehii.github.io; that request includes ordinary web traffic such as your IP address as handled by GitHub Pages.

Retention and deletion

You can delete a single cheer file inside the app. Clear All Data permanently removes cheer photos, drafts, cached media, preferences, purchase ledger records, and unused Match Flares from this installation, then returns you to first-run setup. Uninstalling Terehi also removes its on-device store. Copies you already exported to Photos are not removed by the app; delete those in the Photos app if you no longer want them.

Children

Terehi is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app to store photos of a child if you are not permitted to do so.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Continued use of Terehi after an update means you agree to the revised policy. See also our Terms of Use.
